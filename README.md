# MOSS & MORTAR — Mold living stone into a garden that grows

One stone, one firefly, one verb at a time. Mold living stone to match the ghost blueprint, guide a firefly to water the thirsty moss by moonlight, and calm THE GRANITE WARDEN — ten days and nights, five moonlit puzzles, one granite warden, thirty stars to earn.

A complete original browser game for make.fun best-game-01: five garden pieces to mold, four whisper nights to water, and a three-phase boss molded in flickering moonlight.

Play it: serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome. No network requests, no external files.

- Mouse: drag the stone sideways to shape it (drag right widens, left narrows). Click a neighbouring garden plot to hop the firefly there. Click DONE or press ENTER to finish a mold.
- ENTER: start / next cycle / finish a mold. R: restart. P: pause. M: mute.
- WIN: finish all 10 cycles and calm THE GRANITE WARDEN. LOSE: run out of hearts.
